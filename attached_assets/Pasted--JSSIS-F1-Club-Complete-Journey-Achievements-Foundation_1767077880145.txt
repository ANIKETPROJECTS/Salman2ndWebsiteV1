# JSSIS F1 Club: Complete Journey & Achievements

## Foundation & Early Days

### January 2020 - The Beginning
On 10th January 2020, the JSSIS F1 Club officially began its journey with just 6 enthusiastic students. The club appointed its first incharge and embarked on an exciting new venture filled with dreams and determination.

### February 2020 - First Steps
The 6 founding members were divided into 2 groups and immediately began preparing for the national finals in the rookie category. Club meetings were held every alternate Wednesday during stayback time in the computer lab. Students dove into Fusion software training, PowerPoint presentation skills, car designing fundamentals, and conducted verbal presentations for the intra-school club level competition.

### March 2020 - Unexpected Pause
The first week of March brought an abrupt halt to all activities when UAE schools closed due to the COVID-19 pandemic. All club operations were suspended indefinitely.

### April - May 2021 - Cancelled Dreams
The National Finals 2020 were cancelled due to the ongoing pandemic, marking a difficult period for the young club.

---

## Revival & First Victory

### June 2021 - A New Beginning
F1 in School restarted in hybrid mode, combining online and face-to-face sessions. This phase introduced the 4x4 segment, featuring RC car design, programming, coding, and manufacturing.

**Major Milestone**: YAS in School selected the JSSIS school auditorium as the national final competition venue for the 4x4 championship.

**Historic Achievement**: 22 teams participated in the 4x4 National Championship. JSSIS fielded 2 teams - ACCELERATORS and SPECTRUM. The JSS Girls Team ACCELERATORS won the Terrain Challenge. JSSIS successfully hosted the entire national final event in collaboration with YAS in School. Remarkably, both teams were formed in just 1 month, showcasing extraordinary dedication.

### September 2021 - Expansion & New Ambitions
F1 in School started in online mode as a precautionary measure in the first week of September. Total enrollment reached 18 students as the journey toward national finals preparation began again. All 3 teams started working on Fusion software.

**Team Evolution**: Teams ACCELERATORS and SPECTRUM merged to form TEAM NAUTILUS. This new team set an ambitious goal - to become F1 in School Professional Category World Champions. Stay-back sessions after school hours began in the physics lab, focusing on car designing and coding.

### November 2021 - Building Expertise
Team Nautilus worked on multiple goals simultaneously, learning new tools and ideas for marketing, enterprise, and engineering. F1 in School alumni played an important mentoring role during this crucial development phase.

### January 2022 - Ambassador Program Launch
The club appointed F1 Ambassadors for specific roles: Mukesh, Sai Suhas, Rutish, Swedha, Bhavik Goplani, Raju, and Gayathri. This leadership structure would prove essential for the club's future success.

---

## Journey to National Championship

### May 2022 - First Testing
Team Nautilus conducted their first testing session with successful results. The car was tested without paint, and the team identified various challenges that needed to be addressed.

### June 2022 - The Championship Run

**Pre-Competition Drama**: The final car model was finalized on submission day itself after a day-and-night struggle by the dedicated team.

**26th June 2022 - NATIONAL FINALS DAY**:

The team reached the Abu Dhabi venue and faced immediate challenges. After a 4-hour wait, they completed their pit display with last-minute cutting and fixing at the venue itself. Transportation struggles were managed, verbal presentations were completed, and the pit display presentation was finished on time.

**The Race**: When the race started at 1:30 PM, Team Nautilus was ready. Their car recorded an incredible time of 1.074 seconds - the FASTEST CAR TIMING of the competition and one of the fastest times in YAS history.

**TEAM NAUTILUS WON THE UAE NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP 2022**

The team was felicitated with trophy and certificates by Principal Lata Ma'am. An announcement was made in front of the entire school, and team mentor and engineering ambassador Mr. Mukesh felicitated the team. The first meeting was held toward World Finals preparation with alumni and the Principal, and the World Finals team selection was completed.

---

## Expansion & Multi-Team Development

### September 2022 - Full Operations Begin
The F1 club became fully operational in face-to-face mode. Club activities expanded to include National finals preparation, STEM Lab sessions, and World finals preparation.

**KHDA 2022 Inspection**: Students presented F1 School to KHDA inspectors, showcasing the program's educational value.

**Growth Initiatives**: Nautilus took the initiative for the juniors club. An intra-school competition started, and the school ordered an F1 School track for the premises. Nautilus members participated in events including the Abu Dhabi GP.

### January 2023 - Infrastructure Milestone
The track was inaugurated by CEO Mr. Govindrao Naik. Mr. James, World Finals incharge from YAS in School, visited the track area and held a meeting with NAUTILUS to check world finals progress.

### February 2023 - Inter-School Engagement
An inter-school competition was conducted with winners receiving prizes for reaction time and verbal presentations. Reaction time competitions were held, fostering collaboration with other schools.

### March 2023 - Major Infrastructure & Program Expansion

**Historic Milestone**: The F1 Club received its first official F1 room. The room was equipped through collaboration with STEM partners and technology partners. The club acquired its first resin printer and UV cleaner, making the room fully equipped for future engineers with engineering and manufacturing session capabilities.

**Two Parallel Journeys Started**:
1. World Finals journey for Team Nautilus
2. National Finals journey for Team Mercurial

**Nautilus Junior Programme Launched**: A nurturing program for primary kids with STEM activities began.

**Strategic Division**: Team Nautilus divided their work into two critical parts:
1. Collecting sponsorship money and marketing
2. Conducting innovative events for world finals profile building

### April 2023 - Explosive Growth

**F1 IN SCHOOL JUNIORS** officially started with primary students. An orientation was conducted for all primary kids, and the response was amazing - over 100 students joined for the new academic year. Total F1 club membership reached 140+ students with 15 active alumni members.

**Competition Structure**: The final round of the intra-school competition for rookie teams (verbals and reaction time) was held. Team Nautilus judged the entire event.

**Team Formation**: Team Mercurial split into Team Mercurial and Team Aeolian. Both teams started working together for national finals. Rookie students learned manufacturing and designing techniques while Nautilus continued working with the primary team for the STEM show.

**Intra-School Competition**: 13 teams participated in the intra-school competition, which was conducted in 3 rounds at 3 different events, judged by Team Nautilus and monitored by Lata Ma'am.

**Leadership Meeting**: On 27th April, Lata Ma'am met the entire Team Nautilus for a world finals update and follow-up.

### May 2023 - Distinguished Visitors & Intensive Preparation

**4th May**: His Holiness Jagadguru Sri Shivarathri Deshikendra Mahaswamiji visited the F1 track and interacted with students, blessing their endeavors.

**15th May**: ISRO Scientist Aluru Seelin Kiran Kumar visited the school and interacted with F1 students about their achievements and STEM progress. He showed keen interest in the projects and gave valuable inputs for world finals. The team asked him doubts about speed, friction coefficient, astronomy, and rocket science applicable to aerodynamics. Team Nautilus applied his suggestions in their world finals portfolio.

**Team V3 Established**: A new 6-member team was formed for the 4x4 Development Category for National Finals 2023.

**Four Teams Working Simultaneously**:
- Team Nautilus (World Finals)
- Team Mercurial (National Finals)
- Team Aeolian (National Finals)
- Team V3 (4x4 National Finals)

Team V3 had only one month for national finals preparation but finished basic RC car coding on the first day, demonstrating remarkable efficiency.

**Expert Sessions**: Team Nautilus conducted various STEM learning and engineering events. They invited Mr. Vishakh, a former world finals participant, for an interactive session explaining real-life applications and career opportunities after F1 in School. Sessions were held for secondary students.

**23rd May - NAUTILUS JUNIORS Canister Challenge**: A special event was held for middle school non-F1 students.

**STEM Sessions by Mr. Mukesh**: He conducted two specialized STEM sessions - one for primary F1 students and another for secondary F1 students mentoring Team Mercurial and Team Aeolian.

**30th May**: A meeting was held with alumni and Team Nautilus for world finals decisions per new regulations.

**31st May - Community Outreach**: F1 in School opened to other Dubai schools for collaboration. Schools without tracks were allowed to use the JSSIS facility for car testing.

**31st May - Special Workshop**: A workshop for primary students on manufacturing and designing was conducted. Students prepared Ethara competition car models for national level. Head of Engineering Raksan led the hands-on learning session, while Head of Enterprise Hema Dassani demonstrated designing and marketing.

## National Finals 2023

### June 2023 - Final Preparations
All national final teams completed final registration. YAS F1 in School inaugurated a new experience centre and invited Team Nautilus as chief guests (as reigning National Champions). Mr. James checked their world finals readiness status, and Mr. Nabil gave manufacturing and CNC car tips.

The new YAS experience centre was fully equipped with competition tools. Team Nautilus tested their first prototype for world finals and was interviewed by media, sharing their experiences.

**13th June Onwards - Mock Verbal Presentations**: These began in front of Lata Ma'am and Salman Sir.
- Team Mercurial delivered a confident and amazing verbal presentation
- Team Aeolian had a nervous performance with mistakes that were discussed, and they promised improvement
- Team V3 impressed Lata Ma'am and received feedback for improvement areas

A new deadline was given for car submission. All 3 teams started final touchup and coating with activities including design, graphics, print, paint, polish, shine, sponsorship, weight check, and reaction time practice.

### 15th June 2023 - RACE DAY: F1 IN SCHOOL NATIONAL FINALS

Both professional teams were ready. Lata Ma'am came to wish both teams good luck. The teams reached the venue and prepared their displays. Verbal presentations were completed on time, pit displays finished within the allocated time, and individual races were conducted.

**Stunning Result**: Team Aeolian finished with the BEST TRACK TIME (after the 1st and 2nd fastest teams were disqualified). Team Mercurial jumped from 8th position to 3rd position in the final race. All rounds were completed within time.

### 21st June 2023 - RESULT DAY

Both teams were ready and confident for the results announcement.

**CHAMPIONSHIP VICTORIES**:
- **TEAM AEOLIAN WON NATIONAL CHAMPIONS**
- **TEAM V3 (4X4) WON DEVELOPMENT CHAMPIONS**

### August 2023
Mr. Paul visited the school and handed over the champions trophy in a special ceremony.

## World Finals Journey

### September 2023 - The Ultimate Challenge

During summer holidays, Team Nautilus continued their world finals project work, trying every possible innovative idea. They were particularly strong in marketing, sponsorship, and car design.

**5th September 2023**: Team Nautilus departed for Singapore to represent India and JSSIS at the World Finals.

### WORLD FINALS 2023 - SINGAPORE

Team Nautilus competed against the best teams from around the globe, representing 1.2 million participants worldwide.

**EXTRAORDINARY ACHIEVEMENTS**:
- **8th place overall globally** among all participating teams
- **1st place in Asia** - Champions of the entire Asian continent
- **1st place in GCC region** - Regional dominance
- **4th fastest track time in the world** - Engineering excellence
- **Fastest race time in the entire world** - Ultimate speed achievement
- **Most consistent reaction time in the world** - Precision and practice perfected

Team Nautilus set a high standard for future generations and received global recognition at the 2023 F1 in Schools World Championship.
